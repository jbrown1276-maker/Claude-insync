## Before You Use This Document

Two things to understand about InSync's SEO position:

**1. The domain is a blank slate.** InSync currently ranks for none of its core expertise topics. 230 monthly organic visits. Traffic is driven by an accidental AI blog and brand misspellings. This is not a deficit — it is an opportunity. The competition for every keyword in this architecture is thin. A well-optimized piece on a low-authority domain can reach page one for niche B2B terms within 3–6 months.

**2. Volume looks small — that's correct.** These are specialist corporate L&D terms. "How to measure training effectiveness" gets 170 searches per month. That is the right number. Every one of those searchers is exactly InSync's buyer. Don't calibrate to consumer search volume.

---

## The Core Architecture Principle

Three months. Three distinct keyword clusters. No overlap.

Each month owns a cluster. July's blogs do not compete with August's blogs for the same keyword. All three months point traffic toward the same three cornerstone pages. Over Q3, InSync builds topical authority in measurement, VILT, and live learning outcomes — the three topics it should own.

## The ROI Language Note — Critical

The Q3 plan explicitly replaces "ROI" language with "outcomes/impact/results" throughout all content. This is the right voice decision.

**However:** Semrush shows "training ROI" and "roi training" still get 260–390 monthly searches. People search using ROI language even when InSync's content uses outcomes language.

**The resolution:** Target ROI as a search term in title tags and meta descriptions only. Use outcomes language in all body copy. These are not in conflict — the metadata captures the search, the content delivers the InSync voice.

Example:
- **Title tag:** `How to Measure Training ROI | InSync Training` ← search term
- **Body copy:** "The question is not what your training cost — it's what changed because of it." ← outcomes voice

## Internal Linking Map

This is how the 12 blogs, 3 premium asset pages, and 1 cornerstone VILT page connect to build topical authority.

```
VILT Cornerstone Page
(insynctraining.com/answer-center/virtual-instructor-led-training)
        ↑ linked from all 12 blogs + 3 premium asset pages

July Blogs (4)          August Blogs (4)         September Blogs (4)
     ↓                        ↓                         ↓
Why Go Live           August White Paper          Readiness Strategy
White Paper Page      Landing Page                Tool Page
     ↓                        ↓                         ↓
     └──────────── all link to VILT Cornerstone ────────┘
```

**The rule:** Every blog post contains exactly two internal links:
1. One link to the month's premium asset page (anchor text: keyword-optimized)
2. One link to the VILT cornerstone page (anchor text: keyword-optimized)

No blog links to another blog directly. Traffic flows up to cornerstone pages, not sideways across posts.

## What This Does Not Cover

- **Backlink strategy** — InSync has almost no referring domains. Backlinks are the other half of authority building. Not addressed here because it requires a separate outreach strategy and is out of scope for this pipeline.
- **Technical SEO** — site speed, crawlability, schema markup. Not assessed. If content isn't ranking after 6 months of publishing, technical SEO is the next place to look.
- **Social signals** — LinkedIn and email drive immediate engagement. They do not directly move Google rankings. But they increase the probability that published content gets linked to by others over time.
