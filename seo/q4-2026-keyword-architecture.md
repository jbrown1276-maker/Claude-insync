## 3. P0 — Keyword Cannibalization

**`blog.insynctraining.com/ai-virtual-training-producers` already exists and already ranks.**

It holds position 5 for "virtual training producer" and position 24 for "virtual classroom
producer" — the same terms the MVP course page holds at 1. Two InSync URLs are already splitting
the same query.

The November 2 blog, `blog.insynctraining.com/ai-producer-superpower`, is **AI + the producer
role** — the same topic as the existing post. Published without a decision, it becomes the third
InSync URL competing for one 110-volume keyword, and the most likely outcome is that it
suppresses the existing post's position 5 without reaching position 1, because position 1 is
already InSync's own course page.

**This must be resolved before publish, not after.** Three options, in order of preference:

1. **Differentiate and interlink (recommended).** Target the new blog at the role-evolution and
   human-advantage angle rather than the role-definition angle. The existing post answers "what
   is an AI virtual training producer." The new post answers "what does the producer do once AI
   takes the logistics." Different question, different query set. Then link the new post to the
   MVP course with keyword-optimized anchor text, and link the old post forward to the new one.
2. **Consolidate.** Redirect `/ai-virtual-training-producers` into `/ai-producer-superpower` and
   carry its links. Cleanest long-term, but it discards a ranking page and needs a 301.
3. **Publish as-is and monitor.** Casey's weekly `domain_organic` check catches the collision
   after it happens. This is the option that costs the position-5 ranking to learn something
   already known now.

## 6. Intent Hazard — "Virtual Producer" Is a Film Term

A `phrase_related` pull on "virtual producer" returns almost entirely film and video production:

virtual production (1,300) · virtual production studio (390) · virtual production training (320) ·
virtual production stage (210) · unreal engine for virtual production (90) · osvp meaning (110) ·
virtual production pipeline (70) · unreal filmmaking (70)

**"virtual production training" carries 320 volume at KD 22 and has nothing to do with L&D.** It
is LED-wall and Unreal Engine filmmaking. Any InSync content that optimizes for the bare phrase
"virtual production" will attract film students.

**Rule for all November copy and metadata:** the qualifier is never optional. Write "virtual
training producer" or "virtual classroom producer." Never "virtual producer" alone in a title
tag, meta description, H1, or paid keyword. InSync's position 3 for the bare term is incidental
and not worth defending.
