## Logo Rules

**DO:**
- Light backgrounds: use full-color logo
- Dark backgrounds: use white/reversed version
- Square or circle formats: use logomark only (no wordmark)
- Give the logo generous clearspace on all sides
- Minimum sizes: Horizontal 2.88″ × 0.5″ / Vertical 1.59″ × 1.062″ / Mark 0.6″ × 0.6″

**DON'T:**
- Place logo on clashing or contrasting backgrounds
- Separate the logomark from the wordmark
- Rotate or angle the logo
- Include the web address inside the logo lockup

---

## Imagery Guidelines

**Preferred environments:** Home office and corporate settings

**Lighting:** Natural light with a cool tone preferred for visual cohesion across all content

**Contrast:** If background has a dominant color (e.g. royal blue wall), ensure the subject pops against it

**Solid backgrounds:** Must align with or complement the brand color palette

**Subjects:** Lively, engaging, diverse in age and ethnicity — inclusive representation across demographics

**Approved stock sources:** Shutterstock, 123RF, Envato
