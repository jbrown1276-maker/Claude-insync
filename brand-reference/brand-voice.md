## The 5 Voice Principles

### 1. Human, not corporate
*Conversational authority — warm but never lightweight*

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| Use natural, conversational language | Default to buzzwords, filler, or empty business speak |
| Acknowledge real-world complexity and nuance | Sound transactional, detached, or overly dry |
| Speak from authority while maintaining humanity | Be overly casual or flippant |

**Instead of:** *"We deliver customized learning solutions aligned to your organizational objectives."*
**Say:** *"We build learning around your people — how they work, what they need, and what they will retain and implement."*

---

### 2. Clear, not complex
*Lead with clarity — never hide behind jargon*

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| Lead with clarity and intention | Over-explain or overcomplicate |
| Break complex ideas into digestible language | Hide behind technical language to sound credible |
| Be direct | |

**Instead of:** *"Leveraging a proprietary framework grounded in cognitive science"*
**Say:** *"Our approach is built on how people actually learn, so it's easier to engage, retain, and apply."*

---

### 3. Confident, not boastful
*Let results and specifics do the heavy lifting*

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| Let experience and results speak for themselves | Overpromise or rely on superlatives |
| Use specifics and proof points to build credibility | Sound defensive or overly self-congratulatory |
| Be an authority | Be arrogant, condescending, or a know-it-all |

**Instead of:** *"We are the best-in-class leader in innovative training solutions"*
**Say:** *"We helped pioneer the live-learning industry in 2000 and have since trained over two million people along the way."*

---

### 4. Flexible, not one-size-fits-all
*Emphasize customization — never sound prescriptive*

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| Emphasize customization and responsiveness | Sound prescriptive or inflexible |
| Reflect different client needs and contexts | Imply there's only one "right" way |
| Underscore capabilities regardless of size or complexity | |

**Instead of:** *"Our proven methodology ensures consistent outcomes"*
**Say:** *"We adapt our approach to fit your teams' needs so you get consistent results in a way that works for your people."*

---

### 5. Practical, not theoretical
*Tie everything to tangible outcomes*

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| Tie ideas to tangible results | Stay abstract or overly conceptual |
| Show how learning translates into real-world action | Prioritize philosophy over outcomes |

**Instead of:** *"We create engaging learning environments"*
**Say:** *"We design learning people actually use, so the return on your investment shows up in how they work every day."*
