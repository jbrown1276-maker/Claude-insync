# Live Learning Impact Diagnostic — CTA Brief for Sarah

**This is the Week 2 primary CTA.** Full source: `00-diagnostic-source.md`. URL: https://info.insynctraining.com/live-learning-impact-diagnostic

## Describe it accurately (this matters — the week is about not overclaiming)
The Diagnostic is a **self-assessment risk diagnostic** for live learning. It surfaces where learning impact is **fragile, masked by effort, or dependent on heroics rather than systems** — where results will fade once people return to work, especially at scale ("The Fade Effect"). The user self-rates three areas — **Design, Delivery, Continuity** — as **Reliable / Inconsistent / At Risk**.

**It is NOT:** a measurement framework, an ROI calculator, a "what can you claim from your data" tool, a technology evaluation, or a facilitator review. **Do not describe it as any of those.**

## How it bridges to Week 2's theme
Week 2's rule: a number does not prove it will repeat under different conditions. The Diagnostic answers the honest next question — **will it hold?** Before you claim an outcome will repeat, check whether the conditions that produced it are reliable or dependent on heroics.

## Approved CTA framings (use/adapt; do not overclaim)
- "A 30% gain does not prove it will repeat somewhere else. The Live Learning Impact Diagnostic shows you whether the conditions behind your results are reliable or dependent on heroics, before you scale."
- "Find out where your live learning is fragile before scale makes it visible. Take the Live Learning Impact Diagnostic."
- "It is a risk diagnostic, not a scorecard. It surfaces where impact depends on heroics instead of systems."
