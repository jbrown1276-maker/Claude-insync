## What it is
The **Live Learning Impact Diagnostic** — an InSync "Learning Impact Measurement Toolkit." A **self-assessment / risk diagnostic** for live learning environments (face-to-face, hybrid, and virtual). Self-scored by the user.

## The core idea — "The Fade Effect"
Most learning does not look like it fails. The session runs as planned and feels productive. Failure happens later, when people go back to work — and it shows up at scale. Application becomes uneven across teams and regions; some behaviors appear briefly, others never show up; weeks later, leaders are explaining results that do not match the experience. The Diagnostic exists to surface **where learning reliability breaks, and what has to hold for learning to survive beyond delivery, especially before you scale.**

## Purpose & scope
- **Primary definition:** a **risk diagnostic.** Surfaces where learning impact is fragile, masked by effort, or dependent on heroics rather than systems.
- **Core objective:** test whether live learning produces outcomes that **hold once people return to work** — whether design, delivery, and support work together to protect impact.
- **The shift:** from evaluating learning as an **Event** to evaluating it as a **System**. What it evaluates: **structural fragility vs. individual heroics.**

## How it works
For each of three sections, the user gives a **single judgment**: **RELIABLE** (holds consistently as scale increases) · **INCONSISTENT** (works in some cases, not others) · **AT RISK** (breaks or degrades as volume, velocity, or reach grows). Use the prompts to inform the judgment; do not score individual bullets.

**Section 1 — Design for Learning Impact (intent that holds):** whether learning is built to hold up after the session ends. Post-delivery expectations defined; outcomes describe observable behavior under real work conditions; reinforcement and practice designed; uneven application anticipated; responsibility for sustaining learning is clear.

**Section 2 — Delivery & Facilitation (execution that holds):** whether delivery reliably supports learning without relying on facilitator heroics. Quality repeatable across facilitators/sessions/formats; focus, pacing, psychological safety protected; engagement structurally embedded, not improvised; production/coordination function as infrastructure.

**Section 3 — Support & Learning Continuity (conditions that sustain change):** whether the system stays present long enough for behaviors to take root. Post-delivery support resourced; reinforcement at meaningful intervals; accountability for application shared; coaching/reflection extends into daily work.

## Interpreting results
**Patterns across sections matter more than individual gaps.** Sustainable impact occurs only when all three reinforce one another under real-world conditions. Failure patterns: Strong Design + Weak Delivery = inconsistent experiences; Strong Delivery + Weak Continuity = short-lived impact; Strong Continuity + Weak Design = reinforcement of fragile learning.

## What it IS / IS NOT (important for accurate copy)
- **IS:** a way to surface hidden risk before scaling · a tool to support decision-making (not to justify past investment) · a starting point for strengthening learning systems.
- **IS NOT:** **a measurement framework** · a technology evaluation · a facilitator performance review.
