If the White Paper page does not currently have a high-quality landing page tailored to paid traffic, Quality Scores will be suppressed and CPCs will be higher than benchmarks. Flag to Casey as a pre-launch dependency.

**Only run if the pixel-based retargeting audience size exceeds 1,000 unique users.** Below that threshold, the Google audience is too small for meaningful optimization. Monitor audience size by Wednesday July 15 — if the audience is below 1,000, hold GDN budget and reallocate to LinkedIn.

## 6. Landing Page Requirements

The White Paper page (`insynctraining.com/why-go-live`) is the primary conversion destination for all paid traffic.

### Required Elements Before Paid Launch

| Requirement | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|
| Headline matches ad copy framing | Google Quality Score + conversion alignment. |
| Above-the-fold form or clear CTA | Paid visitors will not scroll to find the download. Form or CTA button must be visible without scrolling on both desktop and mobile. |
| Form length: 3–5 fields maximum | First name, last name, email, job title, company. Do not add phone number at this stage — this is a content offer, not a sales qualification. |
| Trust signals | Logo, one or two client or industry credentials, brief description of what the White Paper contains. |
| Mobile optimization | At least 40% of LinkedIn traffic will be mobile. Page must load in under 3 seconds on mobile, form fields must be tap-friendly, CTA button must be full-width on small screens. |
| Conversion tracking pixel | Google Ads conversion tag + LinkedIn Insight Tag must fire on the form confirmation page (the thank-you page after submission, not the landing page). Without this, the retargeting audiences cannot be built and optimization is manual. |
| Page speed | Target under 2.5 seconds on desktop, under 3.5 seconds on mobile. Run through Google PageSpeed Insights before launch. |
| No navigation menu (optional but recommended) | Removing the main site navigation from the paid landing page keeps users on the conversion path. |

**Flag to Casey:** If the White Paper page does not currently have conversion tracking pixels installed, this is a blocking dependency. Paid campaigns should not launch without it.

## 7. Stat and Claim Credibility Assessment

Note: Jeff has confirmed all four primary stats are approved for use. This assessment documents source quality and credibility risk for the paid context — headlines and ad copy face higher scrutiny than long-form email.

---

### Claim 1: "$1,254 per employee in direct learning costs"
**Source:** ATD State of the Industry, 2024
**Source status:** Attributed, credible. ATD's annual State of the Industry report is the most cited benchmark in L&D. The correct source to cite.
**Credibility risk:** Low. L&D leaders know the ATD report. Many cite it themselves.
**Recommended action:** Use as written in all formats including ad headlines. Citation format is correct.

---

### Claim 2: "3.7% training spend decline — from $101.8 billion to $98 billion"
**Source:** Training Orchestra / industry data
**Source status:** Attributed but moderate authority. Training Orchestra is a workforce scheduling software company — not a research institution.
**Credibility risk:** Medium. A CLO who knows the industry will ask where Training Orchestra gets that number.
**Recommended action:** Client-approved for use. In ad copy headlines, round the figure: "Training spend fell nearly 4% in 2024." The decimal precision looks overclaimed when the source is a vendor blog. In long-form copy (email, newsletter), keep as written with the parenthetical attribution — the qualification "(Training Orchestra / industry data)" is honest.

---

### Claim 3: "64% of organizations say improving learning measurement and analytics is top priority"
**Source:** Brandon Hall Group, 2025
**Source status:** Attributed, credible. Brandon Hall Group is a recognized L&D research organization.
**Credibility risk:** Low-Medium. Brandon Hall is respected with this audience. The "top priority" framing on a list-based survey can be questioned — include "out of their top priorities" context where character limits allow.
**Recommended action:** Use with Brandon Hall attribution. In LinkedIn ad headlines, frame the claim first, source in body text.

---

### Claim 4: "35% of L&D professionals say it is very difficult to track performance and return of training programs"
**Source:** BizLibrary, 2025
**Source status:** Attributed, vendor-funded research. BizLibrary is a training content library — not a neutral research body. Sample may skew toward their customer base.
**Credibility risk:** Medium-High. A skeptical CLO will recognize BizLibrary as a vendor. Vendor-funded research reads as motivated data to this audience.
**Recommended action:** Do not use in paid ad headlines or primary ad copy. Use the ATD ($1,254) or Brandon Hall (64%) stats in paid creative instead. Keep BizLibrary in long-form email/newsletter body copy where the attribution is visible and readers can weigh it. In a 90-character LinkedIn ad headline, a vendor-sourced stat undermines the credibility the campaign is building.

---

### Claim 5: "40% of talent development professionals rate themselves as proficient at evaluating impact"
**Source:** Referenced in Elena's brief and Marcus's strategy. Sarah identifies source as ATD 2025 in production notes.
**Credibility risk:** Low if ATD attribution is confirmed and included.
**Recommended action:** Confirm ATD 2025 as the source before using in any paid copy. If confirmed, use with attribution. This stat did not appear in Sarah's v2 copy — no paid adaptation needed at this time.
