## 4. Pain Point Framework

### Operational
- Reporting on completions because it's what the LMS captures — not because it's meaningful
- No system for tracking behavior change after a program ends
- Measurement built for compliance reporting, not performance evidence

### Emotional
- Defensiveness when executives question learning's value
- Doubt about whether current programs are actually working
- The quiet discomfort of not knowing if training made a real difference

### Organizational
- Executive decisions made without meaningful learning data
- Budget conversations that feel like justifications rather than investments
- L&D teams unable to point to clear before-and-after performance evidence

---

## 5. Key Messages

**Primary Message:**
Completion rates tell you what happened. They don't tell you whether it mattered.

**Supporting Messages:**
- Most companies measure completion and satisfaction. InSync measures engagement quality, behavior change, and performance outcomes
- The metrics most teams track were designed for compliance reporting — not performance evidence
- The Why Go Live White Paper makes the case for what better measurement actually looks like
- InSync measures differently — and that difference is what makes outcomes visible to leadership
- You don't need more data. You need the right evidence.
