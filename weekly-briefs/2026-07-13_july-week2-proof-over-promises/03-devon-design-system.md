# Week 2 Design System — Proof Over Promises
**Client:** InSync Training
**Week:** July 13, 2026 (Week 2)
**Prepared by:** Devon, Art Director
**For:** Production Team

---

## 1. Visual Concept Summary

Week 2 is a diagnosis, not a pitch. The campaign says: the instrument was wrong — completion rates were built for compliance, not learning. Visually, this lands as a shift from tension to clarity. Where Week 1 held the gap open with Golden Honey signaling a break, Week 2 begins to close it. Cool Blue enters as momentum made visible — the alternative taking form. Every asset carries this arc: something that was measured wrong now being measured right. The palette is colder, more precise, more like a measurement tool than a marketing campaign. The gap motif is the central structural element — not decoration. The typography is tighter, more definitive. The feeling is: the room has gotten quieter and more serious.

| Color | Hex | Role | Usage Rules | Never |
|-------|-----|------|-------------|-------|

Must feel intentional — each use means something. | Never gradient. Never use as a full-bleed background. Never scatter decoratively. One or two meaningful uses per asset maximum. |

**Usage hierarchy:** InSync Blue dominates (backgrounds). White is the primary text layer. Cool Blue is used with restraint — two intentional touches per asset maximum. Medium Blue fills secondary structure. Light Blue appears once per asset if at all.

## 3. Typography System

**All type is set in Montserrat. Fallback: Open Sans.**

| Variant | Usage | Size Range | Weight | Rules |
|---------|-------|------------|--------|-------|
| Montserrat Bold | Hero headlines, stat numbers | 36–72px (scaled to asset) | 700 | One Bold headline per asset. That one element is the entire statement. Never stack two Bold lines at similar scale. |
| Montserrat Medium | Subheads, three-measure framework labels, pillar names, section headers | 18–28px | 500 | Supports the Bold headline, never competes. Keep to one or two lines. |
| Montserrat Regular | Body copy, captions, CTA button labels, metadata text | 14–18px | 400 | Max 65 characters per line in designed assets. CTA labels: 14–16px, all caps, tracked +50. |

**Hierarchy principle:** Every asset has exactly one Bold headline. It is the one thing you read first. Everything else narrows from there. When the stat is the headline, the stat is Bold — context labels around it are Medium or Regular.

**Campaign-specific rules:**
- Headline word counts: 3–6 words preferred. If it runs longer, break it to two lines with the stronger phrase on line 1.
- Numbers always Bold, same size as or larger than the accompanying headline text.
- No italic anywhere in this campaign — this is a diagnostic, not a narrative.

## 4. Core Visual Motif — The Closing Gap

**What it represents:** The distance between where L&D measurement is today (completion tracking) and where it needs to be (behavior change, performance outcomes). Each week, the gap closes. Week 2: gap visibly smaller than Week 1, but not yet closed.

**Two states for Week 2:**

**State A — Gap Partially Closed (primary):** Used in newsletter header, LinkedIn posts, email banner.
- Horizontal line, full asset width, 8px stroke weight at 1200px scale (scale proportionally for smaller assets)
- Left segment: InSync Blue filled bar, approximately 55–60% of total width
- Gap: 10–14% of total width, empty (transparent or white depending on background), slightly narrower than Week 1's gap
- Right segment: Cool Blue filled bar, 28–32% of total width — the alternative taking form
- Bar height: 8px at hero scale. At stat card scale: 4px. At email banner scale: 6px.
- Gap is center-to-center on the canvas horizontally, placed at 60% of vertical height (below headline, above CTA or body copy)

**State B — Gap Nearly Closed (aspirational):** Used in CTA sections, White Paper promotion unit.
- Same construction as State A
- Gap narrows to 5–7% of total width
- Left segment: 62–65% InSync Blue
- Right segment: 32–35% Cool Blue
- Signals: we're closer than you think

**Construction rules:**
- The gap is always a clean break — no gradient bridging the two segments
- The right (Cool Blue) segment always enters from the right edge
- Never add a third color to the gap itself
- Never animate the gap in static assets (animation only in Remotion video version if commissioned)
- At thumbnail scale (below 400px width): simplify to a 2px rule — the gap is still visible but the proportions can be approximate

**What the motif must never look like:**
- A progress bar (no rounded ends — hard, flat terminations)
- A split tone background (this is a line, not a color wash)
- A decorative divider (it always appears with a label or headline nearby to give it meaning)

## 5. Photography and Imagery Direction

**Scenario:** Data in use. A measurement instrument — abstract or literal. Before/after bar comparisons. Clean data visualization. Not people in training rooms, not handshakes, not anything that reads "corporate learning catalog."

**Lighting:** Slightly cool. High contrast. Studio-quality or cleanly rendered abstract/data illustration. No warm-toned filters, no golden hour, no soft diffuse ambient light.

**Subject behavior:** If humans appear: quiet focus, looking at data, not at the camera. No smiling at the lens. No group shots.

**Color grading:** Pull warmth down slightly. Blues and slates should feel accurate, not stylized. If an abstract data viz is generated: use InSync Blue and Cool Blue as primary chart colors. White for grid lines. No color that reads as warm.

**Preferred visual directions:**
- Abstract bar chart with a Cool Blue fill replacing an InSync Blue column — the transition visualized
- Clean before/after split: left panel InSync Blue tones, right panel Cool Blue filling in
- Gap-closing arc as a data visualization, not an illustration
- Empty measurement grid — the tools are there, the right metric is missing (Week 2 narrative)

**Never use:**
- Group training sessions, classroom settings
- Generic handshakes or business meeting stock
- Photos with visible warm gradients or orange tones (Golden Honey is retired; warm tones undermine Week 2)
- Illustrated characters or friendly icon people
- Any image that reads as cheerful or aspirational — Week 2 is a quiet indictment

## 7. Layout Principles

1. **White space is structural, not empty.** Every asset has a breathing margin — minimum 72px on hero assets (1200px wide), minimum 32px on compact assets (600px wide). Do not fill margins with supporting content.

2. **One Bold element per asset, no exceptions.** The headline or stat number is the single visual anchor. If two elements compete at Bold weight, the asset has no hierarchy and no argument.

3. **The gap motif earns its position.** It appears below the headline, never above it. It separates the argument from the action. It does not decorate — it structures.

4. **Cool Blue is a signal, not a backdrop.** It fires in CTA sections, data callouts, and gap-fill bars. When it appears, it means something is changing or actionable. Never use it to fill large areas.

5. **Left-align all body text and headlines.** Centered type is reserved for stat cards only (number + context label). Everything else: left-aligned, hanging from the left margin.

6. **Before/after panels get equal real estate.** When a contrast comparison appears, neither side dominates. Split is 50/50 or the asset is not doing its job.

7. **The bottom quarter of every asset belongs to the logo and the CTA.** No headline copy, no body copy, no secondary motifs below 75% of vertical height — except in the three-measure diagram asset where content extends further.

---

## 8. What to Avoid

**Gradient on Cool Blue.** Cool Blue is a solid signal color. Gradients make it decorative. It must remain precise and intentional. InSync Blue to Medium Blue gradient is permitted (one max per asset) — Cool Blue is never in a gradient.

**Golden Honey in any form.** Not as a divider, not as a data point, not as an accent. Golden Honey is fully retired. Any appearance breaks the Week 2 visual arc.

**Rounded corners on the gap motif bars.** The gap is a clean break between two states. Rounded ends read as a progress bar or a friendly UI element. Hard, flat terminations only — this is a diagnostic, not an achievement tracker.

**Busy stat cards.** The number is the design. Do not add supporting charts, decorative frames, or multiple supporting lines. If the stat card has more than four distinct text elements, it has too many.

**Symmetrical two-column brochure layouts.** Assets split into equal left/right text-and-image columns read as a marketing brochure. Asymmetry (one dominant element + supporting material at a different scale) creates the right tension.

**Friendly illustration or icon sets.** No rounded people, no lightbulb icons, no checkmarks in circles. The visual language is measurement-precise — diagrams, not decorations.

**Warm color contamination.** No image with amber, orange, or warm-grade tones. Photo selection must hold the cool, precise temperature of the campaign.
