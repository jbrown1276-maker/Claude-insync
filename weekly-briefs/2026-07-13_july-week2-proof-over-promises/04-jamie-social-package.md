## 1. Platform Strategy Summary

### Platform in Play: LinkedIn (Primary and Exclusive)

**Why LinkedIn only:**
The primary audience — CLO, VP of L&D, Director of Learning, HR Leadership — lives and works on LinkedIn. This is where L&D practitioners process industry arguments, share frameworks with peers, and signal professional identity. No other platform reaches this audience at the density needed for a three-post sequential argument to land in a single week.

**Algorithm and format considerations that affect execution:**

**External links in post body are deprioritized.** LinkedIn's feed algorithm suppresses reach on posts containing external links in the body copy. For every post this week, the link moves to the first comment. This is not optional. It is the difference between 2,000 impressions and 8,000.

**Native text posts with a strong first line outperform link-share formats.** All three posts are native text plus static image — not link previews. The image is uploaded directly. The link goes to the first comment.

**Character display:** LinkedIn shows approximately 210 characters before the "see more" truncation on desktop. The hook for each post must earn the click before that line. All three posts clear this.

**Optimal posting window for this audience:** Tuesday through Thursday, 8:00–9:30 AM ET. L&D leaders check LinkedIn before their first meeting, not during lunch. Do not post after 10 AM ET for maximum organic reach.

**Dwell time matters more than clicks.** For Posts 1 and 2, the strategic goal is engagement quality (likes, comments, saves) more than link clicks. Saves in particular signal the algorithm that content is worth distributing. Post 3 is the conversion post — link clicks to the video or White Paper are the metric that matters there.

## 4. Hashtag Strategy

**Overall approach:**

Three hashtags maximum per post. In this niche, hashtag volume is not a reach multiplier — it is an audience filter. The goal is to surface this content to L&D practitioners who are actively tracking measurement, impact, and talent development conversations, not to cast the widest possible net.

**Selection logic by post goal:**

- Discovery posts (Post 1) prioritize high-volume community tags (#LearningAndDevelopment, #TalentDevelopment) because the goal is reach with the broadest relevant audience. #TrainingROI is added as a high-intent filter to pull practitioners who are actively working this problem.

- Proof posts (Post 2) lead with intent-specific tags (#LearningImpact) because the goal is engagement quality over reach. Practitioners searching #LearningImpact are already past the awareness stage.

- Conversion posts (Post 3) introduce the program-specific tag (#LiveLearning) for the first time. This reaches the live/virtual learning practitioner community without naming InSync directly. It also begins building the tag association that will carry into Weeks 3 and 4.

**What to avoid:**

Do not use generic tags (#HR, #Leadership, #Business, #Management). They inflate impression counts with non-qualified audiences and suppress engagement rate, which signals the algorithm that content is irrelevant. Engagement rate in an L&D niche post should reflect L&D practitioners, not passive scrollers.

Do not use promotional tags (#L&DConsulting, #Training, #LearningDevelopment with typos or variants). These do not have established communities.

Do not rotate tags randomly. Consistency across the week builds cumulative association and helps the algorithm understand what conversation this content belongs to.

## 5. Engagement Response Guide

### Comments engaging with the argument

**Signal comments** (practitioners saying "this is exactly what I'm experiencing"):
Respond within 2 hours during business hours. Acknowledge the specific situation they named. Add one sentence that extends the argument — do not repeat the post copy. Do not pitch.

Example: "Yes — and what makes it harder is that most reporting templates still default to completion first. The infrastructure shapes what gets measured."

**Framework questions** (asking how to apply the three measures):
Respond within 2 hours. Validate the question. Give a brief, specific answer (2–3 sentences). If the question is substantial enough to warrant a deeper conversation, close with the consultation soft ask: "That's worth a real conversation. We can talk through your specific program — insynctraining.com/insync-training."

**Webinar questions** (asking about a July webinar):
Redirect to the consultation CTA. Example: "We don't have a webinar running this month, but we do offer 30-minute strategy conversations for exactly these questions. insynctraining.com/insync-training"

**Skeptical comments** (questioning whether behavior change can actually be measured, or whether this is realistic):
Do not get defensive. Acknowledge the challenge directly. The correct register is peer-to-peer, not brand-defensive.

Example response to "measuring behavior change sounds good but is impossible to do at scale": "You're right that it's harder than completion tracking. The White Paper gets into what's actually workable at different program sizes — not every method fits every context. Worth a read if you're figuring out what's feasible for your team."

### What to save and share during the campaign window

**Save:** Posts from L&D practitioners sharing their own executive conversation stories. These are social proof for the problem InSync is naming.

**Like and comment:** Posts from thought leaders in the L&D measurement space (ATD contributors, Brandon Hall analysts, Bersin researchers). This builds community signal during the week the campaign is live.

**Share:** Do not share competitor content. Do not share content that contradicts the campaign argument. Share only if the content directly supports the "measurement gap is real" narrative.

### When to escalate to Jeff vs. handle independently

**Handle independently:**
- Positive engagement, thank-yous, follow-up questions about methodology
- Skeptical but good-faith comments from practitioners
- Requests to be connected with the InSync team (direct them to the consultation link)

**Escalate to Jeff:**
- Comments from named competitors or their employees
- Media or analyst inquiries
- Any comment that could be interpreted as a legal or compliance challenge
- Negative comments from current InSync clients or named organizations
- Any comment that names a specific person at InSync in a negative context
