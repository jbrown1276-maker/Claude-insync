## 6. Landing Page Requirements

**Checklist download page:**
- **Copy alignment:** Headline must match ad framing. Not a generic "download your free checklist."
- **Form length:** First name + email only. Every additional field cuts conversion rate. Collect additional fields in a follow-up email.
- **Trust signals:** Jennifer Lindsay-Finan's name and title. InSync's program experience. Verified claims only — no stock testimonials.
- **Mobile optimization:** Fast load (under 3 seconds). Form usable on a phone. This audience encounters ads on LinkedIn mobile.
- **Delivery:** Deliver the checklist immediately after form submit — direct download or instant email. No "we'll send it soon."
- **Conversion tracking:** HubSpot form submission + Google Analytics goal event + LinkedIn Insight Tag installed on page. All three needed for attribution reconciliation.

**Webinar registration page:**
- **Copy alignment:** Headline leads with what attendees leave with, not what they'll "learn."
- **Deliverables prominent:** Breakdown points list, action plan, practical tools visible above the fold. Designer Self-Assessment called out as a giveaway.
- **Speaker credibility:** Practitioner credentials, not corporate bio language.
- **Form length:** First name + email + optional "What's your role?" (facilitator/designer/producer). 3 fields maximum.
- **Conversion tracking:** Same stack as checklist page.
