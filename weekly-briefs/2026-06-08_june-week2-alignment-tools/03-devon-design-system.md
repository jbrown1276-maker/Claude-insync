## 1. Visual Concept Summary

This campaign looks like a working document in the hands of someone who finally knows what to do with it. Clean, precise, deliberate — not polished for the sake of polish. Every asset carries the visual weight of a practitioner tool: structured, legible, purposeful. The dominant feeling is resolution. The checklist is the visual anchor. Golden Honey marks it on every surface. InSync Blue holds the authority. Every asset from email to LinkedIn to video end card reads as one coherent system.

---

## 2. Color System

**InSync Blue — #032b43**
Role: Authority and structure.
Usage: All email/newsletter primary backgrounds, all video end cards, all asset headers.
Must never: appear at less than 80% opacity, pair with Medium Blue as background combination, appear as decorative element.

**Cool Blue — #0883cf**
Role: Action. The only color that asks the reader to do something.
Usage: CTA buttons exclusively. Hyperlinks in email and newsletter body. Interactive elements only.
Minimum button size: 180px × 48px.
Must never: appear decoratively. If it is on screen, it is clickable or it is a mistake.

**Light Blue — #00b9f5**
Role: Energy accent.
Usage: Section dividers in newsletter (4px horizontal rule). One callout highlight per asset maximum. Checklist callout box left border accent (4px).
Must never: fill a background, appear in body text, or appear alongside Golden Honey on the same visual element.

**Golden Honey — #eda113**
Role: The checklist's color. The tool's visual signature this week.
Usage: All checklist callout box backgrounds (15% opacity over white, full-saturation #eda113 left border at 4px). Checklist preview graphic badge. "New this week" label. Blog 2 callout boxes.
Must never: appear as CTA button color, headline color, or in any context unrelated to the checklist tool.

**White — #FFFFFF**
Role: Structure. The grid. White space is load-bearing — do not fill it.

**Slate Grey — #6e8699**
Role: Secondary text, captions, metadata. Never body copy.

---

## 3. Typography System

**Montserrat Bold** — All headlines, no exceptions. 28–40px desktop, 22–28px LinkedIn graphics, 18–22px mobile.
**Montserrat Medium** — Subheads, checklist section headers, newsletter section labels. 16–20px.
**Montserrat Regular** — All body copy. 14–16px minimum. Never below 14px.
**Montserrat Bold (small)** — CTA button labels. All caps. Letter-spacing 0.05em. 14px buttons, 12px secondary links. Button labels are verbs only.
**Open Sans Regular** — Email fallback only when Montserrat fails to render.

Hierarchy principle: Three levels maximum. Headline → Subhead → Body. If a fourth level appears, simplify the content.

---

## 4. Core Visual Motif — The Handoff Arrow

What it represents: The moment design intent moves from designer to delivery team.

Construction: Single-weight horizontal arrow (2px stroke, #032b43 or white). Left node: "Design" in Montserrat Medium. Right node: "Delivery" in Montserrat Medium.

States:
- **Broken (drift):** Arrow interrupted with a gap in the middle. No label. Two nodes disconnected.
- **Aligned:** Arrow complete, uninterrupted. Small document icon at midpoint (the checklist traveling with intent).
- **Active (end card/LinkedIn):** Arrow animated with single pulse left to right. One cycle. Stops on aligned state.

Geometric spec: 24px arrowhead, 2px stroke weight. No drop shadow, no gradient, no 3D treatment.

Scale: Hero/newsletter 320px min | LinkedIn 200px min | Email callout 120px centered | Video end card 280px white on InSync Blue.

Must never look like: a process diagram, a funnel, a roadmap, a corporate flowchart.

---

## 5. Photography and Imagery Direction

**Scenario:** Team at the handoff moment — designer and facilitator across a document together, producer reviewing annotated run-of-show, someone marking up a printed checklist. Working moments, not staged collaboration.

**Lighting:** Natural light, slightly warm. No harsh overhead lighting.

**Subjects:** Looking at the document, not the camera. Working, not performing. Karen Vieth only for direct-address to camera.

**Color grading:** Slight desaturation (+10% neutral, -5% saturation). No filters or presets.

**Backgrounds:** Home office or mid-corporate. No open-plan offices with branded graphics, no glass-walled conference rooms.

**Never use:** Stock smiles at laptops, training room with whiteboard presenter, aerial shots around a table, hands on keyboard representing productivity, any image where the primary subject is a screen.

**Karen Vieth video:** Direct address to camera. Slight forward lean. Neutral background — home office bookshelf or plain wall. Indistinguishable from a well-lit Zoom call. That is the goal.

## 7. Layout Principles

1. Single column always — email and newsletter. No sidebars. No exceptions.
2. The checklist block is the visual center of every piece it appears in — largest in email, first in newsletter, hero in LinkedIn Post 1.
3. CTA buttons are the only Cool Blue elements. If Cool Blue appears somewhere that is not a button or link, it is an error.
4. Spacing is content: 24px between major sections, 16px between related elements, 8px between label and content. Do not collapse to fit more content — cut content instead.
5. Three levels of type hierarchy maximum. If a fourth appears, simplify.
6. Golden Honey marks the checklist and nothing else. If it appears on a webinar element, video thumbnail, or decorative border — correct it.
7. Video thumbnails use left-aligned text panels, not overlaid text. Split layout: image left, text panel right.
8. The handoff arrow appears in isolation — one per asset maximum. Multiple arrows read as a process diagram.

---

## 8. What to Avoid

- **Split-screen comparison layouts** — read as software demos, not practitioner tools
- **Checklist as app UI mockup** — show as a physical printed document, not a digital form
- **Golden Honey outside checklist context** — dilutes its role as the tool's visual signature
- **Leadership and authority imagery** — silhouettes, summit metaphors, aerial office views
- **Montserrat Light in body copy** — use Regular as the floor; Light reads as aspirational
- **Gradients** — brand palette is flat; gradients undermine the precise, tool-like quality
- **Drop shadows on checklist graphics** — show as a document, not a floating card
