## What to Brief Differently

**Always instruct Sarah to use article titles, not internal shorthand.**
Add to Sarah's invocation: "Reference any third-party article by its full title and author name in reader-facing copy. Never use internal shorthand like 'Blog 2' or 'the article.'"

**Add preview text to Sarah's channel spec by default.**
Newsletter spec should always include: Subject line + Preview text. Add "Preview text (1 line, max 90 characters)" as a required deliverable for the newsletter channel in every invocation going forward.

**Confirm article title before invocation, not after.**
Karen Vieth's blog title was available in `00-blog2-source.md`. The orchestrator should pass the confirmed article title (not just the file) to Sarah's invocation so there's no ambiguity in what to call it.

**Devon SVG production is a separate invocation.**
Design system approval does not mean SVGs are built. Week 2 SVGs have not been produced yet. Brief Devon for SVG production explicitly after Jeff approves the design system. Do not conflate spec approval with file delivery.
