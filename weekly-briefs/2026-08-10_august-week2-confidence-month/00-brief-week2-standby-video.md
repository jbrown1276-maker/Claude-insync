## 4. Pain Point Framework

**Operational**
- Learner feedback collected after every session but never translated into leadership-level evidence
- Survey data that sits in a report no executive ever reads
- Gap between what facilitators observe and what makes it into a performance dashboard

**Emotional**
- Feeling that the evidence you have isn't 'executive-ready' even when it's rich and meaningful
- Frustration at presenting data that gets dismissed as anecdotal
- The gap between knowing a program worked and being able to prove it

**Organizational**
- CLO asks for impact data; L&D provides survey scores
- No shared language between practitioners and executives about what 'good' looks like
- Learning data collected in silos with no pathway to organizational performance reporting
