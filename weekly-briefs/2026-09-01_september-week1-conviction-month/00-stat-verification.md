## 13% vs 24% — RESOLVED (not an error — two different questions)
- **13%** = data sources *relevant to business outcomes* (broad).
- **24%** = data tied to an *explicit, specific organizational-goal metric* (narrower phrasing). A separate 24% = "integrate engagement / task-performance / behavioral-change data."
- **Blog 1 (JLF edits) leads with 24%. The CB leads with 13%.** Both correct, but they read as the same claim to a reader.
- **DECISION NEEDED / consistency rule:** lead with **13% "relevant to business outcomes"** as the blunt gap against 94%. If 24% is used, phrase precisely as "tied to a specific organizational goal" so it's clearly a different point. Do not present 13% and 24% as interchangeable.
