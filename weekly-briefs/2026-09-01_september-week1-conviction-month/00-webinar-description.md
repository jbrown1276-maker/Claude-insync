## Description

Clients are asking for live learning again. In fact, 41% of training providers are seeing renewed interest in classroom learning after years of digital-first delivery. But here's the challenge most L&D teams face: they are collecting evaluation data and doing nothing with it. Satisfaction scores get filed away. Completion rates go on a slide. And the program stays the same for the next cohort.

What separates organizations that actually improve their live learning from those stuck collecting data is simpler than you think: they use the right evaluation framework and they focus their effort on data that drives design decisions.

In this session, we'll walk through five fundamental shifts in how successful organizations think about evaluation. You'll see real examples of programs that moved from "did they like it?" to "what will we change?"—and how that one mindset shift led to measurable business outcomes.

Whether you're designing, delivering, or evaluating live learning, you'll leave with a practical framework and the exact tools you need to turn evaluation data into program improvement.

## What You'll Walk Away With

- **Using Evaluation to Improve Live Learning: A Companion Guide to the Research** — how the 5 Shifts apply when you're designing virtual, hybrid, and in-person learning, complete with practical tools and real examples you can use immediately
- **The 5 Shifts framework** — a clear model for rethinking evaluation across design, delivery, and follow-up
- **Practical templates** for facilitators, designers, and evaluation leaders to implement with their next program

## Who Should Attend

- Facilitators and producers delivering programs who could be capturing behavioral data during sessions
