## 1. Visual Concept Summary

Before a single asset is built, picture this: a deep-navy surface with the stillness of a legal exhibit or a research cover. A large numeral sits alone with room to breathe. Directly beneath it, always, a thin teal rule and a small mono citation line — the **source stamp** — so the number and its attribution are one object, never a number with a footnote. When two numbers appear together, the easy one (94%) is rendered in muted desaturated gray and the one that matters (13%) in InSync Cool Blue teal, and the contrast argues the strategy in color before a word is read. This is the look of a case that has been made: quiet, editorial, evidence-first, no decoration spent where it does not carry meaning. Teal is rationed — it appears only on what matters and on the CTA, so its arrival always reads as "this is the thing." White space is treated as a material. The system must look identical at hero scale and at LinkedIn-thumbnail scale, because we assume every asset is seen small first.

## 2. Color System

Four roles, no fifth hue. The palette is not decoration — it is the argument. Muted gray = the easy data everyone collects. Teal = what matters. Navy = the ground of proof. White = room to think.

| Color | Hex | Role this month | Usage rules | Must never |
|-------|-----|-----------------|-------------|------------|
| **Deep Navy** | `#032B43` (see Sec. 9, Flag A — confirm exact brand navy with Jeff) | Primary ground. Authority, proof, "on the record" surface. Hero backgrounds, evidence exhibits. | Dominant field on nearly every asset. Pair only with white/off-white type, muted-gray data, teal signal. | Never used as an accent on light grounds; never gradient-blended into teal; never lightened into a "friendly" blue. |
| **InSync Cool Blue (Teal signal)** | `#00B9F5` | THE signal color. Reserved for what matters: the 13%, the business-outcome figure, the exception, the turn, the CTA, the source-stamp rule. | Appears sparingly and always with meaning. When teal is present, it names the payoff. Source-stamp rule is the one systemic teal element. | Never used for decoration, backgrounds, borders-for-borders'-sake, or the 94% figure. Never more than one "payoff" teal moment competing per asset. |
| **Muted Gray** | `#8A97A6` (cool desaturated navy-gray) | The "easy data everyone collects." The 94% satisfaction figure, completion rates, filed-away metrics. | Always the visual foil to teal. Set the muted number and the teal number at the same size so the difference reads as *meaning*, not hierarchy. | Never brighter or more saturated than teal. Never used for a CTA. Never so dark it disappears on navy. |
| **White / Off-White** | `#FFFFFF` pure / `#F4F1EA` off-white | A material, not a background. Interpretive/body type on navy; the breathing room around numbers. | Off-white for large calm fields and "exhibit paper" framings; pure white for crisp type and fine rules. Generous margins mandatory. | Never crowded. Never tinted toward yellow (reads as Golden Honey ghost). Never used as a CTA fill (CTA is teal). |

**Usage hierarchy.**
- **Dominant:** Deep Navy ground + white/off-white type. 80%+ of every surface.
- **Accent:** Muted Gray (the 94 / easy-data register) and the small persistent teal source-stamp rule.
- **Reserved:** Cool Blue teal as the payoff — the 13, the turn, the CTA. Spend it once or twice per asset, never more.

**Retired / banned:** Golden Honey `#eda113` — do not use in any capacity this month. No third accent color. No red/alarm palette (this is conviction, not fear).

## 3. Typography System

Confident and editorial. Large numerals are the heroes. Running copy is **sentence case** throughout (matches Sarah's rule).

**Face 1 — Data / Display / Headline (clean grotesque sans).** Recommended Inter or Archivo (tabular/lining figures required). Hero numeral 180–320 px on hero assets (down to ~64 px at thumbnail); headline 48–72 px; subhead 28–36 px. Numerals Bold/800; headlines Semibold/600; subheads Medium/500. Principle: the number is always the largest element on the asset.

**Face 2 — Interpretive voice (optional humanist).** Source Serif or Newsreader for interpretive one-liners. 24–40 px, Regular/400–Medium/500. The serif is the *voice*; the sans is the *evidence*. Never let the serif carry a number.

**Face 3 — The Source Stamp (mono / small-caps).** IBM Plex Mono or Space Mono, or Face 1 small-caps with wide tracking. 11–16 px (never below 11 px final; target ≥13 px on LinkedIn). Small, quiet, always present, never omitted.

**Month rules.** Sentence case for all headlines/subheads/body. Tabular figures mandatory where numbers stack/compare. No all-caps except the optional small-caps source register. Tight letter-spacing on hero numerals, open on the source stamp only.

## 4. Core Visual Motif — The Source Stamp

**What it is.** A fixed citation mark beneath every published figure: a thin teal rule with a small mono/small-caps citation line under it. The visual embodiment of "on the record" — number and attribution as a single inseparable object. The through-line that makes all assets read as one system.

**Geometric construction.** Hairline teal rule `#00B9F5`, 2–3 px at hero (1.5 px min at thumbnail), width = optical width of the numeral above or a fixed 240 px. Rule 16–24 px below the numeral baseline. 8–12 px gap, then the citation line: `InSync 2026 industry survey of L&D evaluation practices, Dr. Charles Dye, N=200 + 24 interviews.` Optional left-aligned exhibit tick (6 px teal square). Left-aligned to the numeral's left edge by default; centered only on the hero card if symmetrical.

**Three states (track the arc).**
1. **Recognition:** stamp under a question or a muted-gray figure. Thin, short teal rule. (Tuesday LinkedIn.)
2. **Evidence:** stamp under the stark contrast (94 vs 13). Rule spans both; one shared citation for the pair. (Hero card, newsletter, teaser.)
3. **Conviction:** stamp under the teal payoff (the 13, the turn, the CTA). Full teal weight — the seal on a made case. (Thursday LinkedIn, late nurture, CTA blocks.)

**Never.** Not a footnote floated away from its number. Not a logo lockup. Not a badge, ribbon, seal, or award medallion. Not decorative. Not gray (the rule is always teal). Never omitted or abbreviated past legibility.

**Scaling.** Hero: rule 2–3 px, citation 14–16 px, full attribution. Thumbnail (~150 px): rule 1.5 px min, citation ≥13 px, attribution may compress to `InSync 2026 survey · Dr. Charles Dye · N=200+24` but source, researcher, and sample must survive. Squint-test every asset at 150 px; fix the numeral, never drop the stamp.

## 5. Photography and Imagery Direction

**Scenario.** No photographic scenario. The research is the imagery. Favor abstract data-and-evidence treatments: honest charts, exhibit-like framings, the artifact-of-record feel. Think "cover of a credible research report," not "marketing hero." No human subjects this month.

**Grading.** Navy-dominant, teal-signal, muted-gray data. Flat, calm, high-legibility. No drama lighting, no softening gradients, no glow. Off-white "paper" fields allowed for exhibit framings.

**Charts, if any.** Real, defensible representation of the verified data only. Muted gray for the easy/common series, teal for the what-matters series. No invented trend lines, no chart junk. Every chart carries a source stamp.

**Never shoot or source:** generic stock (teams high-fiving, lightbulbs, gears, handshakes); mocked-up product dashboards / InSync software screenshots (trips the self-report trap); red/alarm imagery; any number without its source stamp; "17 industries" or "14% long-term outcome data" (unverified).

## 7. Layout Principles

1. The number gets the room — hero numeral always the largest element.
2. The source gets its fixed place — stamp directly beneath every figure, same treatment everywhere, never a footer.
3. The CTA gets teal, and teal gets the CTA — teal anywhere else steals meaning from the payoff.
4. Muted vs. teal must read before the words do — squint-test at thumbnail.
5. White space is a feature — min 80 px margins on hero; one idea per asset.
6. One primary CTA per asset — secondary actions are text links.
7. Design for the save, not the like — self-contained, source baked in, screenshot-clean.
8. Thumbnail-first — build at hero, validate at 150 px before sign-off.

---

## 8. What to Avoid

- Golden Honey `#eda113` or the Q3 incompleteness/completed-line motif — a closed chapter; this is a new one.
- A fourth accent color — breaks the color-as-argument logic.
- Fake dashboards / product screenshots — reads as a vendor demo, trips the self-report trap.
- Alarmist treatment (red, danger, shock, warning icons) — conviction, not fear.
- Any stat without its source stamp — non-negotiable.
- "17 industries" / "14% long-term outcome data" rendered anywhere — unverified.
- Conflating 13% and 24% — 13% = "relevant to business outcomes" (lead); 24% = "tied to a specific organizational goal" (distinct). If 24% is used, its label must say so.
- Generic stock imagery — the research is the imagery.
- Stamp-as-badge — it is a citation, not a seal/ribbon/medallion.
- Logo louder than the number — logo at or below the scale of the source stamp on stat assets.
- Canonical asset titles or a named webinar host.
