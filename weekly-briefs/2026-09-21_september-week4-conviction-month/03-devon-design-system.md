## 1. Visual Concept Summary

"Session to System" — the close. For three weeks teal was a single mark that changed meaning: a rule under a number (W1, *who says so*), a spine binding a number to its conditions (W2, *what it proves*), a thread accumulating five behaviors into one protocol (W3, *what you watch for*). Week 4 does the one thing the prior three were building toward: **the marks connect.** The isolated teal marks of the month resolve into one continuous line that climbs, sharpens, and finishes — the visible argument that individual pieces compound into a system.

The look is unchanged: deep-navy exhibit surface, teal rationed, white space as material, one idea per asset, thumbnail-first. What changes is the register of the teal itself. In prior weeks teal *arrived* on the thing that mattered. This week teal **arrives resolved** — no longer a signal placed on a number, but a completed through-line that has run the length of the month and closed. Emotional register: arrival. The month was building; now it is built.

Two things carry the whole week and neither is negotiable. First, the system line **compounds — it does not loop.** The design→deliver→reflect→refine→repeat cycle is the referent, but a flat circle that returns to start would argue the opposite of the week. The line ascends: each iteration returns to the same phase one step higher and one degree sharper, a rising build, not a wheel. Second, the line **resolves** — by the hero composition it is one unbroken, connected, calm, complete system. Case made and closed.

And this week — by Jeff's override — the system gains a human face. A deliberate mix of authentic, navy/teal-graded live-learning people (generated with `gpt_image_2`) and the graphic system line, balanced so the set reads as one system with a human face, never a stock deck. Navy still governs 80%+ of every surface, including behind every photo-real image. This is the "more human, not less" close.

## 4. Core Visual Motif — The System Line

The close of the source-stamp lineage. Where W1 placed a rule, W2 a spine, W3 an accumulating thread with nodes, **W4 connects the marks into one continuous resolved line** — the month's four moves assembled into a single build. A sibling to every prior motif, not a new one.

### 4a. What it is
A single teal connective line reading as the month's argument assembled: four segments (the four weeks / four moves — proves, exact, already, compound) joined into one unbroken polyline that **ascends and sharpens** across the composition and terminates in one resolved teal endpoint. Beside it, detached, sits the isolated gray element (the one-off session). The line is the argument; the gray element is the foil.

### 4b. Construction (geometry)
- **The line.** Teal `#00B9F5`, one continuous stroke. Weight builds along its length: **enters thin (~2 px, the faint W1 register) and finishes full (3–4 px hero, crisp)**, so the stroke visibly sharpens start to close. Four connected segments meeting at three junctions (the month's hand-offs, W1→2→3→4). Each junction sits **higher than the last** — the line gains altitude left-to-right.
- **How it COMPOUNDS (non-negotiable 1).** Render iteration as *improvement*, never a flat closed loop. An **ascending ratchet / rising-step read**: the design→deliver→reflect→refine→repeat cycle returns to the same phase each turn but one step *up* and one degree *sharper* — a line that climbs as it repeats. Concretely, four ascending segments each crisper than the last, whole line trending upward. Acceptable: a rising stepped line, an ascending open spiral read left-to-right (a staircase, not a wheel), or four linked upward strokes each steeper than the last. **A circle that returns to its start is banned.**
- **How it RESOLVES (non-negotiable 2).** By the hero composition, one unbroken, connected, calm, complete system, terminating in **a single stronger resolved teal terminal** (a filled node or clean sealed endpoint — the "case made and closed" seal, descended from W3's card-8 resolved form). No loose ends. The feeling is arrival: the eye lands on the terminal and rests.
- **The isolated one-off (gray foil).** A single **detached gray element** — one short severed segment or one disconnected node — placed apart from the teal line, not touching it, visibly *not part of the system*. The good day that reverts. Same size register as a single teal junction so the difference reads as *connected vs isolated*, not hierarchy. Gray never touches the teal line.

### 4c. What it must never look like
- A **flat closed loop / wheel / clock face** → argues repetition, not compounding. Banned outright.
- A **named-framework diagram** (labeled 5-stage Live Learning Formula wheel, InQuire three-dimension chart) → inverts the hierarchy, reads as a product tour. The line is the *vocabulary* of compounding, never a methodology diagram with callouts.
- A **decorative border or flat baseline rule** → it must ascend and sharpen, or it is ornament.
- A **progress bar** → the line is an argument about accumulation, not a fill state.
- **Louder than the thesis sentence** on the flagship → the sentence is the hero there.

### 4d. Scaling / thumbnail
At 150 px the line must read as (a) one continuous teal line, (b) rising/sharpening left to right, (c) resolving into one clean terminal, (d) one detached gray element beside it — before a word is read. Min stroke 2 px at the thin end; resolved terminal never below ~10 px. Squint-test: "one connected build that climbs and finishes" must land in under two seconds. If the ascent reads as a flat loop at thumbnail, rescale the vertical rise first.

## 7. Layout Principles

1. The sentence can get the room — on the flagship, the thesis line is the largest element.
2. The system line ascends and resolves, or it is not the motif — climb, sharpen, finish in one clean terminal; a flat loop is a failure.
3. Isolated gray vs connected teal must read before the words do — one detached gray element beside one continuous rising teal line, in under two seconds.
4. Teal gets the system line and the CTA, nothing else.
5. Navy governs, even behind a face — a generated photo grades into the navy system, never breaks its 80%+ dominance.
6. Balance the human and the graphic across the set — two people-led (newsletter, Post 3), the rest graphic-led; the mix is a requirement.
7. One primary CTA per asset — Email #4 single-ask; secondary actions one-line text links.
8. Design for the save — self-contained, screenshot-clean, leadership altitude held (except Post 3's elevation beat).
9. White space is a feature — min 80 px header / 72 px square margins; one idea per asset.
10. Thumbnail-first — validate at 150 px: "one connected build that climbs and finishes" must land.

## 8. What to Avoid

- A flat closed loop / wheel / clock face for the cycle → argues "repeat yourself." The line must ascend and sharpen.
- Leading any asset with a named-framework diagram → inverts the hierarchy, reads as a product tour.
- Reviving a Week 1–3 hero as a hero (94-vs-13 card, conditioned client-stat card, behavior carousel) → closed loops.
- A conditions bracket around a fabricated client number → none exist; white space and a sharper thesis line instead.
- Rendering Blog 4's four-iteration example as data → hypothetical; label it (Face 1 Medium, not mono) if shown; safest off.
- The "60% decline since 2020" figure anywhere → unverified; never rendered.
- ATD figures attributed to InSync → always `— ATD 2025 State of the Industry Report`; supporting, never a hero.
- Stock-cliché people (high-fives, lightbulbs, gears, staged smiles), off-brand/warm grading, a photo breaking navy dominance.
- A named webinar host, an invented webinar title/takeaways → standing one-line reference only.
- Claiming the Dye link is confirmed, or the Companion Guide free/ungated/gated.
- Golden Honey, a fourth accent, red/alarm, fake dashboards, brand mark louder than content, teal used twice (beyond the line + CTA).
