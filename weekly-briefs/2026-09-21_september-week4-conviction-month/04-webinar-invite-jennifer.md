## Body

Hi [First name],

A few years ago I stopped asking teams whether their programs were good and started asking a harder question. What did you change because of what you measured? Most of the time the honest answer was nothing. Not because people did not care, but because no one had built the practice that turns evaluation into a design decision.

So my team and I documented that practice. It became the research behind the five shifts, and on September 24 we are going to walk through how to actually use it.

This is not a lecture on why measurement matters. You already know it does. It is a working session on the mechanics, how evaluation data moves from a filed report into a redesigned program, and how that redesign shows up as business impact your leadership can see.

Everything I would hand a team on day one comes with it. The companion guide, Using Evaluation to Improve Live Learning, translates the five shifts into real design decisions across virtual, hybrid, and in-person programs. You get the framework itself, and the templates our own designers and facilitators use to put it into practice.

I built this from inside the programs, not from the outside of them. If you are the person responsible for making live learning better, I want you in the room, so you can take it back to yours.
