## 7. Production Notes

**Newsletter — cut strategy:** v6 told the full Cindy Foster story inside the newsletter. v7 selects two of the most powerful observable moments (role play language, breakout improvement) and exits at the moment of arrival. The InQuire dimensions are dropped from the newsletter body — they live in Wednesday's carousel now. The newsletter no longer carries that weight.

**LinkedIn Newsletter Teaser — cut strategy:** v6 repeated the observable moments across three paragraphs from slightly different angles. v7 uses one moment (the group that struggled twice) and exits. The first moment lands. The reader does not need a second and third example to feel the point.

**Wednesday carousel — format rationale:** LinkedIn carousels are uploaded as a PDF (one page per slide). Post copy above the carousel sets the frame and the "Swipe through" prompt. The carousel is self-contained — a reader who only views the slides gets the full InQuire framework. Devon: 6-page PDF, Cool Blue accent system, consistent with Week 4 visual language.

**Carousel Slide 5 — question placement:** The three dimensions (Slides 2–4) build toward the question. Placing it on Slide 5 gives it earned weight. Placing it earlier would make the dimensions feel like footnotes.

**Thursday LinkedIn — 117 words:** Below the 130-word target, but every sentence that could be cut was cut. The sequence is: arc close → hesitation named → clarification → ask. Nothing else belongs. The shorter version is correct.
