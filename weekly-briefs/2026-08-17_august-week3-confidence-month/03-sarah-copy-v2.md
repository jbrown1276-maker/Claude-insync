### Tone Principles Applied

1. **Human, Not Corporate:** "Not from a research desk. Alongside delivery teams, inside the complexity." (Newsletter, Email #3)
2. **Clear, Not Complex:** Three IQF dimensions written as single declarative lines (Wednesday). Fortune 100 case in fragments — no setup, no transition sentence required.
3. **Confident, Not Boastful:** Results stated plainly. Attribution in body. No superlatives. No "we're proud to announce."
4. **Flexible, Not One-Size-Fits-All:** Pillar 2 language in Thursday and Email #3 — the program adapted to the organization's complexity. Not a repeatable module applied uniformly.
5. **Practical, Not Theoretical:** Every channel ties proof to what the reader can do: present to an executive team, download the practice, start the conversation.
