## 4. Pain Point Framework

**Operational**
- No structured approach to measuring all three dimensions of engagement in a live session
- Environmental factors (tech, design, logistics) treated as separate from learning outcomes
- Intellectual and emotional engagement tracked informally, if at all

**Emotional**
- Feeling that measurement is complicated and requires tools the team doesn't have
- Overwhelm at the idea of building a measurement practice from scratch
- Wanting a framework that's practical enough to actually use, not just theoretically sound

**Organizational**
- Measurement initiative stalled because there's no clear starting point
- Different people in the organization measuring different things with no shared framework
- Gap between what the measurement literature recommends and what a busy L&D team can realistically do
