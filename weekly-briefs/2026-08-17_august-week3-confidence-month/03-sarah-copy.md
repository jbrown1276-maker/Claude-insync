### Post Body

A session can look active and still miss the learning outcome. The links work, the breakout rooms open, the chat has enough activity to look encouraging. Then the facilitator asks learners to apply the skill. The room goes quiet.

That is not a learner problem. It is a signal problem.

Environmental engagement: can learners participate without fighting the experience. Intellectual engagement: are learners being pushed to think. Emotional engagement: are learners willing to contribute honestly.

The InQuire Engagement Framework gives delivery teams a shared structure for the observations they already carry out of every session.
