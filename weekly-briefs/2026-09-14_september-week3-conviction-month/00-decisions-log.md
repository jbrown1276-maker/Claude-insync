## Framework anchor
The **observation protocol / five real-time behaviors** — facilitators as the primary evaluation-data source; behavioral evidence captured during transitions/after (3–5 min), not a form that splits attention during teaching. Real-time observation predicts transfer better than post-course surveys. Practitioner register.

## The five behaviors (from Blog 3 — the carousel spine)
1. A manager applies the framework to their own situation, unprompted.
2. A manager asks a clarifying question about a specific scenario.
3. Managers discuss how their current approach differs from the framework (gap-awareness).
4. A manager pushes back or challenges the framework (critical thinking).
5. Learners interact with and teach each other (peer learning).
