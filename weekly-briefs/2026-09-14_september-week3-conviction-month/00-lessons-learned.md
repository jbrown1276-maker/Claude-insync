# Lessons Learned — September Week 3 (Conviction Month · Proof Over Promises)

**Week:** Sep 14–19, 2026 · **Closed:** 2026-07-21 · **Scope:** Small team (Stages 1–3), input = Blog 3 only (no CB)

---

## What worked

**The audience flip was the right call and it held.** Weeks 1–2 were leadership-first; Week 3 inverted to practitioner-first, and Marcus stated the inversion explicitly rather than letting it happen by accident. Because it was named up front, Elena and Sarah both calibrated to the Jen Finan register from the first line, and the deck reads as one voice talking facilitator-to-facilitator. The month now has a deliberate altitude arc: boardroom (W1 research) → boardroom (W2 disciplined claiming) → floor of the session (W3 the practice).

**"Blog only" input worked cleanly.** Jeff supplied Blog 3 plus answers to four questions, no creative brief. Marcus built strategy from the blog + the Week 1/2 continuity, Elena wrote the CB from scratch, and nothing was lost versus the fuller-input weeks. The four-question intake (deliverable set, primary CTA, social split, Devon in/out) front-loaded the decisions that would otherwise have surfaced mid-pipeline.

**The carousel got real, buildable direction.** A format the pipeline had never produced was specced card-by-card by Elena and then engineered by Devon into a reusable template + a **teal-thread device** (enter card 2, accumulate a node per behavior card, resolve on card 8, hand off to the CTA on card 9). The "five behaviors read as one protocol" problem was solved as a design argument, not left as a vibe. This template is now reusable for any future multi-part carousel.

**The objection-killer was planted early on purpose.** "You don't take notes while you teach" is the line that decides whether practitioners adopt or dismiss the practice. Marcus flagged it, Elena seated it on carousel card 2 (not buried), and Sarah stated it plainly in every long asset. The design guards it too: capture is only ever depicted as the after moment, never mid-session.

**Stat discipline is now routine, not heroic.** Saks & Belcort and Brandon Hall attributed to their own sources; the "8 of 12 managers" hypothetical kept out of reader-facing copy entirely (the safest handling); no client numbers invented for a week that had none. The team no longer needs to be reminded of the hazard list every time — it is internalized.

**The Bozarth Post-3 override integrated without friction.** Jeff swapped the planned objection-answer post for a Bozarth-article promo mid-approval. Because the change was logged and passed to Elena and Sarah as binding, the register-shift post landed cleanly (Bozarth byline only, never Hofmann) and actually improved container variety across the LinkedIn set.

## What needed revision / what to watch

**Register drift is the standing risk of a multi-week campaign, and it will get worse in Week 4.** Three straight weeks now: two leadership-register, one practitioner. Whatever Week 4's register is, name it in the first instruction and check the newsletter and Email specifically — those are the two channels that default back to boardroom language on muscle memory. This was flagged pre-emptively in Week 3 and held; do not assume it holds automatically.

**The Companion Guide's identity and gated status is STILL unresolved, three weeks running.** It was flagged in Week 2 and again as Week 3's highest-leverage unblock, and it is now the Week 3 primary CTA with copy written to survive either resolution. This cannot keep sliding. If Week 4 uses it again, resolve it before Stage 3, or the copy and the Email button label stay in a hedge.
→ **Change for Week 4:** get the Companion Guide URL + gated/ungated status confirmed at kickoff, not at publish.

**Two design decisions have been deferred every week and are now overdue.** (1) The Week 1 **hero-card version** (v1 flat / v2 Evidentiary Minimalism / v3 Higgsfield) was never picked, and it governs cross-week visual consistency — four weeks will ship in three different flagship treatments if it is not settled. (2) The **file-production pass** keeps being separated from the design-system pass; it is a known second step and should be budgeted into the week from the start, not discovered at the gate.
→ **Change for Week 4:** pick the hero-card treatment before any Week 4 build, and schedule Devon's production pass alongside his system pass.

**Webinar detail never arrived.** Card 9 shipped as date + URL only because no title, description, or host was ever confirmed. The Sept 24 webinar is now inside Week 3's window and still has no confirmed title. Week 4 is the last week before it; if a title/description exists, it strengthens every remaining webinar reference.

**Asset production for Weeks 2 and 3 has not been run.** Both weeks are approved at the spec level; neither has had its SVG/PNG build. This backlog is accumulating. Before more weeks stack up, decide whether assets get built weekly or in a batch at month-end.
