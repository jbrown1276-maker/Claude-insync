## 8. ESCALATION PROTOCOL

### Tier 1 — Casey Handles
- LinkedIn post publishes late by <30 min: first comment staged, Casey posts immediately
- Graphic renders incorrectly: swap to fallback file, re-publish within 1 hour
- Email rendering issue found in test: fix in platform, re-test, send when clean
- Underperforming ad group: pause and flag to Alex

### Tier 2 — Casey Alerts Jeff Within 2 Hours
- Blog stat publicly challenged in comments or social media
- Google Ads spend reaches $2,000+ with 0 conversions
- LinkedIn Sponsored CPL running above $500
- Email bounce rate above 5%
- Registration page down during live campaign period
- InSync requests last-minute copy or date change

Casey's Tier 2 protocol: Message Jeff with (1) what happened, (2) current impact estimate, (3) recommended response. Jeff decides.

### Tier 3 — Jeff Decides Immediately
- Webinar platform outage day-of (June 25)
- InSync requests to cancel or postpone webinar
- A stat in published content publicly challenged as fabricated
- Any legal, compliance, or PR issue

### Platform Outage Responses
- **LinkedIn down on post day:** Reschedule to next available window same day; if unavailable, next morning. First comment goes live within 5 min of publish.
- **Google Ads dashboard unavailable:** Log actions manually; verify via GA4 traffic.
- **HubSpot down:** Track registrations via webinar platform directly; import retroactively.
