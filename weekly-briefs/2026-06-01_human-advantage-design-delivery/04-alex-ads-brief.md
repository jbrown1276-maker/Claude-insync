## Strategic Framing

Before diving into execution: this campaign has an unusual challenge for paid. The practitioner audience — facilitators, instructional designers, producers, L&D ops managers — is highly skeptical of anything that reads like a product pitch. Marcus flagged this directly. The risk in paid is that ad copy triggers a "vendor" read and loses the audience before they click.

This means the creative strategy for paid must mirror the content strategy: **name the problem, not the solution.** The goal of Week 1 paid is to generate webinar registrations by making the pain visible and positioning the webinar as a peer conversation, not a sales event. Every headline, description, and landing page recommendation below is written through that lens.

**Channel Priority Recommendation:**
1. **LinkedIn Sponsored Content** — Primary channel. The audience is highly defined by job title and function. LinkedIn's targeting is the only platform that can reach IDs, facilitators, and L&D ops managers with precision. No search intent required; the audience IS the targeting.
2. **Google Search** — Secondary channel. Captures active search intent from practitioners already in problem-recognition mode. Keyword volume is modest in this niche, but the intent is high.
3. **Google Display** — Tertiary/remarketing only. Too much impression waste for a niche practitioner audience. Use only for retargeting people who visited the webinar registration page but didn't convert.

### Bid Strategy Recommendation

**Recommendation: Manual CPC with Enhanced CPC — start here for Week 1**

Rationale: This is a new campaign with no conversion history. Google's automated smart bidding strategies (Target CPA, Maximize Conversions) require 30–50 conversions in a 30-day window to optimize effectively. At 30–50 target registrations for the entire week, the algorithm will not have enough data to function. Starting on Manual CPC with Enhanced CPC (eCPC) gives human control while allowing modest machine adjustment on clicks showing purchase intent signals.

**Migration path:** After 50–75 total conversions across the campaign's lifetime (likely by Week 3–4 of ongoing campaigns), migrate Ad Groups 1 and 2 to Target CPA bidding. For now, stay manual.

**Starting Bid Guidance by Ad Group:**
- Ad Group 1 (High Intent): Start at $12–15 max CPC
- Ad Group 2 (Problem Recognition): Start at $8–12 max CPC
- Ad Group 3 (Long-Tail): Start at $6–9 max CPC

### Landing Page Alignment Specs

For ad copy to convert, the webinar registration page must deliver on the exact message the ad set up. If the page contradicts or dilutes the ad copy, click-through will not produce registrations.

**The registration page must contain:**

1. **Above-the-fold headline that mirrors ad copy tone.** Do not use a corporate title like "Design to Delivery Sync Webinar." Use language like: *"The gap between what you designed and what got delivered — let's name it."* or *"A working practitioner conversation on design-to-delivery alignment."*

2. **The problem statement before the CTA.** Don't lead with the registration form. Give the practitioner one paragraph that confirms they're in the right place: *"Most teams have never built a shared system connecting design intent to delivery execution. The rework that shows up in delivery prep isn't a talent failure. It's a systems gap. June 25, we're having the conversation."*

3. **Credibility signal — peer language, not vendor language.** *"This is not a product demo. It's a working conversation."* This sentence must appear on the page.

4. **What they'll leave with** — 2–3 bullet points: what practitioners will walk away knowing or having, not a list of "topics covered."

5. **Short registration form.** First name, last name, email, job title (for list segmentation), organization. Do not add more fields — each additional field reduces conversion rates approximately 10–15%.

6. **Social proof if available.** Attendee count from past webinars, 1–2 practitioner quotes. If none exists, skip — empty social proof sections undermine credibility.

7. **UTM parameter preservation.** Ensure the registration confirmation/thank-you page fires the Google Ads conversion event. If using a third-party webinar platform (Zoom Webinars, Hopin, etc.), verify the redirect or embed fires the pixel.

### When to Optimize vs. When to Wait

**Days 1–2:** Do not optimize. Let the campaign generate impressions. The algorithm (even Manual CPC) needs initial delivery data. Optimizing in the first 48 hours based on early numbers is noise, not signal.

**Day 3:** Review CTR by ad variant. Pause any LinkedIn creative with CTR below 0.2% after 2,000+ impressions. Pause Google Search ads with CTR below 1.5% after 100+ impressions.

**Day 5:** Review cost-per-registration. If above $400, investigate: Is the landing page converting? Is the audience right? Is the CPC too high relative to the budget?

**Day 7 (End of Week):** Full performance review. Carry forward winning headlines, pause losing ones. Prepare Week 2 creative swap to introduce Checklist as secondary CTA.

**Do not:** Change bid strategy, audience targeting, or landing page during Week 1. Week 1 is a learning week. Changes mid-flight invalidate your baseline data.

**Do not use the 42.5% figure without InSync confirming its origin and agreeing to the attribution.**
