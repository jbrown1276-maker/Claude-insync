## 3. LINKEDIN POST — WEDNESDAY (Deepening/Reframe)

This is not a talent problem.

Skilled facilitators run sessions with incomplete briefs. Experienced producers absorb configuration surprises the morning of. Instructional designers hand off work that was never structured to travel well. And everyone moves forward, because moving forward is the job.

The gap between design intent and delivery reality is not a failure of preparation or communication. It is an upstream structural problem. The decisions that determine whether a facilitator can walk into a session ready — whether a producer has what they need before the run-of-show — those decisions happen in design.

Design is the upstream control point. Practitioners who understand that have a different kind of leverage.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Newsletter CTA:** "Read the Blog + Register for the Webinar" is intentionally a two-action CTA because the newsletter audience is warm and the blog builds trust before the ask. If the platform supports a single primary CTA only, prioritize the webinar registration and move the blog link to an inline text link in the body.

**LinkedIn Tuesday and Wednesday:** Neither post names the webinar directly. That is intentional. Tuesday earns the problem recognition. Wednesday earns the credibility. Thursday earns the ask. If Thursday doesn't run, add a light CTA to Wednesday's post.

**Email PS:** The PS echoes the newsletter opening almost intentionally — it should feel like it's in conversation with anyone who read that newsletter. It can be cut without harming the email's conversion path if needed.

**Alignment Checklist tease weight:** Newsletter tease is softest (one line, no specifics). Email tease is most specific about what the checklist does. Thursday LinkedIn splits the difference. The webinar remains the Week 1 conversion point throughout.

**Webinar date — June 25:** All references have been updated from "next week / Week 2" to Thursday, June 25. The end-of-month positioning is used to strengthen the early registration angle across Thursday LinkedIn, email body, and newsletter.

**Tone revision — systems framing:** All language that implied design failed or was the problem has been replaced with the governing reframe: the gap exists because most teams have never built a shared system connecting design intent to delivery execution. Both designers and delivery practitioners are framed as capable people working without that connective structure.
