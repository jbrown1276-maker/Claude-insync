## 3. LinkedIn Post — Wednesday (Deepening/Reframe)

### Post Copy

You are forty minutes into a live session. The chat is moving. Breakout rooms are running on schedule. The facilitator hasn't lost the room.

From the outside, the session looks like it's working.

But you're watching something else. You're watching whether learners are using the language of application — not just restating what was covered. You're watching whether the questions are getting more specific as the session progresses. You're watching whether the practice activity reveals adjustment, or just completion.

These are the three dimensions the InQuire Engagement Framework maps: environmental, intellectual, and emotional engagement. Not as a form to complete after the session. As a lens for what you observe while you're in the room.

The difference between a session that looks good and a session that produces readiness to perform is observable. You have to know what to look for.
