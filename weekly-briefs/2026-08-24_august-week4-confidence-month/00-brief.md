## 4. Pain Point Framework

**Operational**
- Measurement practice feels like extra work on top of already full schedules
- No clear trigger or protocol for when and how to capture session-level evidence
- Data collected inconsistently because there's no simple daily habit to anchor it

**Emotional**
- Wanting to build a measurement practice but not knowing what to do first on a Monday morning
- Feeling that good measurement requires tools and resources the team doesn't have
- The gap between aspiring to data-driven L&D and actually doing it day to day

**Organizational**
- Measurement treated as a project rather than a practice
- No organizational habit of capturing learning evidence at the session level
- Insights lost between sessions because there's no lightweight system to preserve them
