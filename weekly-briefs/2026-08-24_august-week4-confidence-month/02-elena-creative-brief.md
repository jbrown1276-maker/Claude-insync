## 4. Tone of Voice (for Sarah)

### The Week 4 Register: Settled Confidence

Week 4 does not build. It does not explain. It does not persuade from scratch.

The voice this week speaks from a position of earned confidence. The reader understands the measurement gap. They have seen what the IQF produces. They have been offered the White Paper. Week 4 says: we are ready when you are.

The tone is warm and direct. Peer-grade authority — not professorial, not promotional. The reader should feel that the person writing these words has thought carefully about their specific situation and is making a genuine offer.

**What Week 4 sounds like:**
- "Let's look at your program together."
- "Your measurement questions are exactly what this conversation is for."
- "No agenda other than where you are and what you need."
- "That is how InSync works: alongside your team, not in front of it."

**What Week 4 does not sound like:**
- Any sentence that re-explains the measurement gap as if the reader hasn't heard it
- Any re-introduction of the IQF or the White Paper as if they are new
- Urgency language that implies the reader is falling behind or running out of time
- Aspirational framing ("transform your approach," "elevate your practice")
- Vendor tone in any form

### Blog 4 Calibration (Jennifer Hofmann)

Read Blog 4 — *What Does "Making Learning Count" Actually Look Like on a Tuesday?* — before writing any copy for the week. Key register markers to absorb:

- Opens in a specific moment: the facilitator pauses because the group is repeating words but not applying the idea
- Operational specificity: facilitator guides, producer run-of-show, post-session surveys, breakout groups
- Names what practitioners observe but can't yet report: "useful evidence is often present, but unmanaged"
- Multi-cohort contrast: "The dashboard may show both sessions as complete. The learners did not experience the same program."
- The Tuesday Test as a structural device: could your team tell, during an ordinary delivery week, whether the program is working?
- Voice is grounded practitioner-authority — direct, not aspirational

Do not import Blog 4's specific phrases verbatim into channel copy. Absorb the register. Write from it.

The reader at the start of Week 4 is not skeptical. They are deciding.

The reader who says yes to this conversation is the reader we have been writing to all month. The creative tone should honor that. No final push. No urgency pressure. A warm and specific invitation from someone who has done this work and knows it can help.
