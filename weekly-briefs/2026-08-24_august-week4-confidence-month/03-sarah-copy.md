## 3. LinkedIn Post 1 — Tuesday (Discovery/Hook)

The session ran well. Participants were present. The facilitator moved through the material with care, noticed a breakout group that caught its own mistake, saw a moment in the chat where a question shifted from clarification to application.

And then the room closed, the producer logged off, and that evidence stayed in the facilitator's head.

The post-session survey came back positive. The dashboard said complete.

What practitioners observe during delivery is often the most useful evidence in the system. It does not survive the handoff. Not because it is hard to capture. Because no one built the handoff to receive it.

That is the gap. Not in the data. In the practice.
