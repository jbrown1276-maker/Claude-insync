## 2. LinkedIn Post — Tuesday, July 22 (Education)

Repetition means the same slides show up in Boston and Singapore.

Scale means the same standard of preparation, design intent, and outcome focus shows up in both rooms.

Those are not the same thing.

The difference between a live learning program that replicates content and one that replicates outcomes is the infrastructure behind it. The Live Learning Formula names that infrastructure: Design and Development, Delivery and Facilitation, Support and Learning Continuity. Not separate workstreams. The same system, operating in every location.

When those three components work together, the facilitator in Singapore knows why an activity exists — not just which slide it appears on. The learner in Boston gets the same quality of engagement the learner in any other cohort gets. That is not talent. That is design.

Active learners outperform passive learners by more than 50% on knowledge assessments (Docebo, 2025). That gap does not close by finding better facilitators. It closes by building the conditions for active learning into the system before anyone enters the room.

The InQuire Engagement Framework is InSync's method for measuring whether those conditions are actually present. Not attendance numbers. Not poll responses. The quality of engagement that predicts whether learning will transfer.

The Why Go Live White Paper has the full evidence — including case studies from global programs in technology, consumer goods, and manufacturing. Link in first comment.
