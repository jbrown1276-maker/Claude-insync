## What Needed Revision

### Blog reference missing from Sarah's first draft
The week's anchor blog (Jennifer Hofmann) was not mentioned anywhere in v1 or v2 of the copy package.

**Root cause:** Elena's creative brief did not include the blog in her newsletter direction. The blog rule was not in Elena's briefing system.

**Fix applied:** Blog rule added to MEMORY.md and Elena's brief corrected. Going forward: blog must always appear in the week's copy, minimum in the newsletter as a secondary section below the primary CTA.

**Brief Elena explicitly on this for Week 4:** "Newsletter must include a secondary section referencing this week's anchor blog — one teaser line, one link, below the CTAs."

### InQuire framed as a new introduction
Sarah and Elena both treated InQuire as something being introduced for the first time. Jeff caught it. InQuire is an existing InSync framework that has been in use for years.

**Fix applied:** InQuire framing rule added to MEMORY.md and voice-tone-reference.md. Correct framing: "InSync's method for measuring the engagement conditions that convert live learning into performance." No announcement language anywhere.

**Brief all agents explicitly on this for Week 4 and beyond:** InQuire is established. Present it as such.

### White Paper positioned as a launch asset
Week 3's original brief (submitted by Jeff) called it "White Paper launch week." The White Paper has been the primary CTA since Week 1. The newsletter's subject line ("The Why Go Live White Paper is here") treated it as new content.

**Fix applied:** Newsletter restructured — blog as hero, White Paper as answer. Subject line changed to "Outcomes don't scale on talent alone. They scale on a system." The White Paper CTA: "Download it this week. The argument is complete." — directional payoff, not announcement.

**For Week 4:** The White Paper is a standing offer. Frame it accordingly. Week 4's White Paper CTA should be even more direct — we're in the invitation/action week.

### Paid budget assumption wrong by a factor of 16
The $4,000/week figure in Week 2's Casey plan was never confirmed and carried forward unchallenged into Week 3's planning. Actual budget: $850/month (~$212/week). Alex rebuilt his entire brief around the real constraint.

**Fix applied:** $850/month confirmed and locked in MEMORY.md. This figure is the starting point for all paid planning in August and September.

**Alex's Week 3 recommendation:** Full $212/week into Google Search only. LinkedIn paid requires $500–$750/week for meaningful signal — not viable at current budget. Revisit in Q4 if budget scales.
