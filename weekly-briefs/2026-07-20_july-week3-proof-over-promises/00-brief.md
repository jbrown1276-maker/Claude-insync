## 4. Pain Point Framework

**Operational**
- No structured framework for designing live learning that consistently produces outcomes
- Delivery quality varies across facilitators and regions with no common standard
- No way to connect program design decisions to downstream performance results

**Emotional**
- Uncertainty about whether current program design reflects best practice
- Wanting to make a stronger case for live learning but lacking the evidence
- Feeling underprepared when asked to justify live learning investment to leadership

**Organizational**
- Live learning treated as an event, not a system
- No shared organizational standard for what quality facilitation and design looks like
- Format decisions made by executives without evidence to guide them
