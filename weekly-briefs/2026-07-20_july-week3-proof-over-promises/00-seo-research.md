## The Headline Finding

InSync is essentially invisible in organic search for every topic they are expert in. The site drives 230 monthly visits from 508 keywords — almost none of which are related to training measurement, VILT effectiveness, learning ROI, or live learning outcomes.

Their current top traffic drivers:
- A blog about natural intelligence vs. AI (captured an accidental trending query)
- Managed learning services content
- Brand misspellings ("instnc," "insync university")

**The implication for Proof Over Promises:** The four July blogs are the strongest on-topic SEO content InSync has ever produced. They are not optimized for search. Small adjustments to metadata, titles, and internal linking could establish InSync's first real footprint in measurement-adjacent keywords — without changing a word of body copy.

## Audience Search Behavior — What They're Actually Asking

From the `phrase_questions` pull on "training measurement":

| Question | Volume | Signal |
|----------|--------|--------|
| how to measure training effectiveness | 170/mo | The primary question — moderate volume, clear intent |
| how to measure effectiveness of sales training programs | 70/mo | Vertical-specific — sales L&D audience |
| how to measure ROI for training | 50/mo | Budget-defense intent |
| how to measure the effectiveness of a training program | 30–40/mo | Same question, different phrasing |
| how do you measure training effectiveness | 40/mo | Conversational variant — shows up in featured snippets |

**What this tells Marcus and Sarah:** The audience is searching "how" questions, not "what" questions. They already know measurement is a problem. They are looking for a method. This aligns exactly with the Week 3 arc — from naming the gap (Weeks 1–2) to proving the solution exists (Week 3). The copy that answers "how do you actually measure engagement quality?" is also the copy that captures these searches.

## Competitor Landscape

Semrush's organic competitor list for InSync is composed of small niche players with minimal keyword overlap. The recognizable names — trainingindustry.com, td.org, elearningindustry.com — do not even appear as InSync's competitors because the keyword overlap is near zero.

**What this means:** InSync is not losing rankings to ATD or Training Industry. They simply are not present in the same keyword universe. The field is open.

**One data point on Training Industry:** They rank position 5 for "training measurement" (90/mo). InSync doesn't rank at all. A single well-optimized blog post could take that position — Training Industry's page is a glossary entry, not a substantive piece. InSync's Jennifer Hofmann blog is substantive.

## What to Leave Alone (July)

The four July blogs are pre-written and pre-approved. Body copy does not change. What CAN be optimized without touching copy:

1. **Title tags** — if the CMS allows separate title tags from H1s, optimize for target keywords
2. **Meta descriptions** — write these for click-through, include the target keyword
3. **Internal links** — link from blog posts to the White Paper landing page using anchor text like "measuring training effectiveness" rather than "download the White Paper"
4. **Blog URL slugs** — if not yet published, confirm slugs include target keywords (e.g., `/measuring-training-effectiveness` rather than `/blog-3-hofmann`)
