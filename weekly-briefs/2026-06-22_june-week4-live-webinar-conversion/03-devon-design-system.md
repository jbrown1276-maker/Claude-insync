## 1. Visual Concept Summary

"The Proof Becomes Practice" looks like a campaign that has arrived somewhere. Weeks 2 and 3 built a visual system of drift and alignment — diverging lines, converging evidence, before states in warm grey, after states in cool blue. Week 4 keeps that grammar intact but shifts the emotional register: we are no longer making the case. The case is made. Pre-webinar assets carry a single warm signal — "Today" — that tells the audience the moment they've been building toward is here. Post-webinar assets drop the urgency entirely and replace it with forward motion: clean, unhurried, specific. The infographic is the week's signature visual — a five-step arc that makes the whole month visible in a single glance. Every other asset lives in its shadow.

## 3. Typography System

**Montserrat Bold**
- All display headlines (H1 level): 32–48px depending on asset format
- Email subject preview text in image assets: 20–24px
- Blog 4 title: 40px
- Infographic step labels: 16px white on InSync Blue nodes
- Banner text ("Today," "Replay Available"): 28–36px depending on banner dimensions
- Never used for body copy or CTA labels

**Montserrat Medium**
- Subheads (H2–H3): 18–24px
- Section labels in infographic: 13px, InSync Blue
- Callout captions below pull quotes: 13px
- Email section divider labels: 14px
- Never used for primary headlines or body paragraphs

**Montserrat Regular**
- All body copy: 15–16px, optimal line-height 1.6
- CTA button labels: 14–15px
- Text link labels: 15px, colored per link type (Cool Blue for ebook/checklist/assessment links; Ocean Blue for audit link)
- Newsletter body: 15px, max 560px text column width in email

**Hierarchy principle:** The eye moves from Bold headline → Medium subhead → Regular body. No weight skipping. Never use Bold for body copy to create emphasis — use Golden Honey treatment or a Medium callout block instead.

## 4. Core Visual Motif

**The handoff arrow**
A single directional arrow (→) connecting "Design" to "Delivery." Line-weight only — 2px stroke, InSync Blue, no fill, no shadow. Arrowhead: simple filled triangle, proportional to stroke weight. Carries forward from Weeks 2 and 3 unchanged.

**States:**
- Standard: 2px InSync Blue stroke, white background
- On dark background: 2px white stroke
- In infographic connectors: Cool Blue stroke, 2px, with filled arrowhead

**What it must never look like:** No 3D treatment. No gradient. No decorative flourish. No rounded endpoints. It is a working diagram element, not a decorative graphic.

**Scale rules:**
- Blog hero and infographic: full connectors at 24–32px total height
- Email header: not used as standalone — appears only within structured infographic
- LinkedIn posts: appears as part of the infographic; do not isolate as a standalone motif at social crop

**Drift vs. alignment grammar (Week 3 carry-forward):**
- Drift: two parallel 2px lines beginning parallel and diverging (~15° spread over 120px horizontal)
- Alignment: two parallel lines beginning slightly apart and converging to a single point
- Used in Blog 4 before/after section. Appears at 80×48px in table context.

**Vertical icons — all at identical pixel weight (1.5px stroke, no fill, InSync Blue), fixed 24×24px inline / 32×32px as section marker:**
- Healthcare: simple cross (+)
- Tech: circuit board trace (horizontal and vertical paths with node dots, simplified)
- Manufacturing: hard hat (dome shape with brim, no detail)
- Energy: lightning bolt (single stroke zigzag)
- Financial services: ascending bar chart (3 bars, left-to-right ascending)

## 5. Photography and Imagery Direction

**Scenario:** Two or three practitioners — a facilitator, a designer, or a producer — working together at a table or shared screen. They are looking at documents: a run-of-show, a checklist, a slide deck with handoff notes. The work is real. The documents are visible and legible enough to be recognized as workplace tools.

**Lighting:** Natural or warm office light. No studio lighting that removes environmental context. Slightly warm color temperature.

**Subject behavior:** Focus and collaboration. People pointing at or annotating documents, not looking at the camera. No smiling at the camera. No performative "team meeting" poses. The subjects are working, not performing work.

**Color grading:** Slightly desaturated overall. InSync Blue tones in the background or clothing are acceptable. Avoid oversaturated stock-photo warmth.

**Never use:**
- Stock smile photos
- Whiteboard and marker enthusiasm
- Tablets or laptops as the primary "learning" symbol
- Empty conference rooms with people gesturing at blank screens
- Any image that reads as generic "teamwork" or "corporate collaboration"

## 7. Layout Principles

1. **One focal point per layout.** The infographic for Blog 4. "Today" for day-of assets. "Replay Available" for post-webinar headers. Nothing competes with the focal point.

2. **Earn the CTA.** No CTA button appears in the top half of any email or newsletter. The Cool Blue button is always the final action in the layout.

3. **Zone 1 is visually uninterrupted.** Post-webinar assets open with prose. No banner break, no button, no icon grid interrupts Zone 1. The Golden Honey acknowledgment treatment is the one exception — it is typographic, not a visual container.

4. **Ocean Blue is a quiet signal.** The audit link sits below everything else. Minimum 24px vertical gap from the nearest Cool Blue element. Never a button. Never loud.

5. **Generous white space is the container.** Minimum 32px padding on all email content edges. Minimum 48px between major content sections. Never compress to fit more content.

6. **Mobile-first for email.** All email assets designed at 600px, readable at 375px mobile. Cool Blue button full-width on mobile (90% of container). Body text never below 14px.

7. **The infographic travels.** Build the Blog 4 infographic at 2400×1260px master and export down. All node labels must be legible at 50% size before the master is approved.

8. **Consistency before creativity.** Week 4 is an established system. Any deviation from the Week 2–3 visual grammar must be justified by this brief, not aesthetic preference.

## 8. What to Avoid

**Animated countdown timers.** The "Today" banner is warm, not aggressive. Any animated element adds urgency pressure that is off-brand for this campaign.

**Exclamation marks on "Replay Available."** The replay is equal-value access. Punctuation that signals excitement undermines that framing.

**Audit CTA as a button.** Ocean Blue is a text link. A button would compete with the Ebook CTA and collapse the hierarchy.

**Ivory Mist outside Blog 4.** This color signals the before/drift state. Using it post-webinar sends the wrong message.

**Cool Blue and Ocean Blue adjacent.** Two blues in close proximity read as a color system error. Always separate them by at least one visual element.

**Stock imagery defaults.** Any photo that could illustrate "teamwork" generically fails the north star test.

**Filled handoff arrow.** The core motif is line-weight only. A filled arrow becomes a brand mark and loses the analytical register.
