## Email JH — Jennifer Hofmann
**Send:** May 21–26 · L&D Leadership Segment

**Subject:** Why delivery inconsistency starts upstream of delivery

---

Hi [First Name],

Here is a pattern L&D teams encounter consistently: a program launches well, but by the third or fourth cohort, something has drifted. Different facilitators are running different sessions. Learner experience varies by region. The team adds a QA layer and refreshes the facilitator guide. Scores stabilize — but never fully recover.

The standard intervention stays at the delivery level. More facilitation prep. More observation. More coaching after the fact.

The evidence points upstream.

When design intent doesn't travel with the materials — when the handoff between designer and delivery team runs on assumption rather than documentation — the ceiling on a session's consistency is set before the first facilitator logs in. The improvement loop stays at delivery because that's where the symptoms are visible. The cause is earlier.

This is what Design to Delivery Sync addresses. On June 25th at 2pm ET, practitioners will work through the specific points where design-to-delivery alignment breaks — and the workflows that close them before launch.
