## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Persona: Practitioners
- **Roles:** Facilitators, Instructional Designers, Producers, Learning Operations Managers
- **Pressure:** Delivering consistent live learning across regions, time zones, and platforms while managing last-minute fixes and rework caused by unclear handoffs and misaligned design intent
- **Emotional State:** Frustrated by delivery breakdowns that feel avoidable, wanting a shared system rather than heroics, seeking practical tools they can use immediately
- **What They Want:** Clear frameworks, shared standards, practical workflows, and peer-validated approaches to reducing rework and improving consistency

### Secondary Persona: L&D Leadership
- **Roles:** CLOs, Directors of Learning Operations, VPs of L&D, Senior Learning Leaders
- **Pressure:** Ensuring delivery quality scales reliably across regions and programs without adding headcount or escalating rework costs
- **What They Want:** Evidence that alignment between design and delivery produces measurable consistency improvements, and a clear path to operationalizing that alignment

---

## 3. Week Strategic Goal

Complete June's conversion arc. Practitioners who have followed the month leave with a clear system (checklist + webinar workflows + Designer Self-Assessment) and a path to applying it with InSync's support (audit). Leaders who engaged with the Leadership Series secondary content have the alignment model framed at the strategic level.

Post-webinar tone shifts to warmth and follow-through. The audience did the work — acknowledge it before pointing to next steps. The audit offer should feel like a natural continuation, not a sales pivot.

## 4. Pain Point Framework

### Operational
- Webinar attendees leaving with insight but no clear organizational pathway to apply it
- Replay viewers who miss the live session needing the same quality of follow-up as live attendees
- Teams who completed the checklist but haven't identified where their specific programs need the most attention

### Emotional
- The post-event dip — inspired during the session but unsure where to start when back at the desk
- Wanting the alignment work to compound beyond a single webinar
- The frustration of great professional development that doesn't translate into organizational change

### Organizational
- No structured pathway from webinar attendance to program-level application
- Gap between the alignment framework learned in the session and the specific programs that need it most
- Leaders who need the business case for investing in alignment audits after the webinar
