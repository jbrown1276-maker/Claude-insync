**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**PORTSMOUTH, N.H., April 16, 2026** — InSync Training is proud to announce that it has been named to the 2026 Training Industry Top 20 Training Companies list for Staffing and Temporary Resources for the third consecutive year. This recognition affirms InSync Training's sustained commitment to delivering high-quality, facilitator-led learning across in-person, hybrid, and virtual environments.

Training Industry, the leading research and information resource for corporate learning leaders, annually identifies top providers in critical sectors of the corporate training marketplace. Selection criteria for the 2026 list included scope and quality of staffing roles and resources offered, market presence, brand visibility, innovation and impact, strength of client portfolio and customer relationships, and business performance and growth trajectory.

> "Winning this three years running isn't something we celebrate with a press release stat. It's a signal that organizations are choosing partners who are accountable for outcomes, not just delivery. Anyone can staff a training program. What's harder — and what actually moves performance — is finding people who take responsibility for what happens after the session ends. That's what our practitioners do. That's what this recognition is about." — *Karen Vieth, President, InSync Training*

What sets InSync Training apart is not simply the breadth of roles it staffs, but the standard those practitioners bring to every engagement. Guided by the Live Learning Formula™ and the InQuire Engagement Framework™, InSync's facilitators, instructional designers, producers, and program managers are trained to drive genuine learner engagement — not just content delivery — across in-person, hybrid, and virtual environments. Whether supporting a single high-stakes program or managing an enterprise learning operation end-to-end, InSync integrates into client teams with the consistency and quality that long-term partnerships require.

For L&D professionals looking to strengthen their own practice, the InSync Academy offers certificates, workshops, and the Virtual Learning Coach™ program — practical development pathways for training practitioners navigating today's complex learning environments.

And for organizations focused on leadership development, InSync's recently established Authorized Partnership with Blanchard extends that same facilitation excellence to globally recognized programs including SLII® — delivered by InSync experts and integrated directly into leadership pathways and internal academies.

Organizations ready to elevate their learning function — whether through expert staffing, leadership development, or practitioner training — should connect with InSync's team.
